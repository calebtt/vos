## Claim
UID injector against a one-file fixture `AudioRecord` app on the S-01 hello image: VOS can inject PCM into another app's live mic stream (`MIX_ROLE_INJECTOR` + `RULE_MATCH_UID` + `createAudioTrackSource`, from a product priv-app with `MODIFY_AUDIO_ROUTING`).

## Procedure
1. Requires S-01 passed (hello priv-app with `MODIFY_AUDIO_ROUTING` granted).
2. Fixture app records with `MIC`, `VOICE_RECOGNITION` and `VOICE_COMMUNICATION` at 16 and 48 kHz, saving WAVs to app storage (pulled to the desktop, never into the repo).
3. Hello priv-app registers an `AudioPolicy` with an `AudioMix` (`MIX_ROLE_INJECTOR`, `RULE_MATCH_UID` = fixture UID) and plays known speech through `createAudioTrackSource`.
4. Transcribe the fixture's recordings with an offline ASR; compute WER.
5. Owner talks over the injection: 20 recordings; count recordings containing the owner's words (replacement vs mixing).
6. A second recorder app records at the same time; confirm it hears none of the injected audio; record which rule matched (UID, session or preset).
7. Timing: record active → first injected sample; tap → record active. p95 of each.
8. Repeat with a classic BT headset connected and HFP active.

## Pass criteria
- Fixture records with all three sources at 16 and 48 kHz.
- Offline ASR WER ≤10% on the injected speech.
- 0 of 20 recordings contain the owner's words (else record "mixes" as a finding).
- Second recorder hears none of the injection; matching rule recorded.
- Record active → first injected sample ≤50 ms p95; tap → record active ≤400 ms p95.
- Repeated with BT HFP active.
