# research/

**Raw AI-model output, kept for the record. These are not reviewed docs.**

The files here are reports, reviews and critiques written by AI models (Claude, Grok, Qwen) while VOS was being planned, and the prompts that produced some of them (`PROMPT-*.md`). They are kept as written, so the reasoning behind a plan decision can be traced back to its source. Only workstation paths and host names were replaced by generic placeholders.

- **Not authoritative.** They can be wrong, out of date, or contradicted by later decisions. Nobody has checked their claims, sources, numbers or recommendations.
- **The plan decides.** [`docs/TARGET-PLAN.md`](../docs/TARGET-PLAN.md) is what VOS actually follows. Where the plan cites a report here, the plan's own text is the decision, and the report is background.
- **Not maintained.** A file is not updated when the plan changes.
